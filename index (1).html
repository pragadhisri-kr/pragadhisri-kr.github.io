Multimodal GraphRAG for Documents (DocGraph)

Featured project · Team project at SRM Institute of Science and Technology

A document question-answering system that turns PDFs into a Neo4j knowledge graph and answers questions with page-cited, chunk-grounded answers.

PDF→Chunks with page provenance→Gemini entity extraction→Neo4j graph→Cypher retrieval→Cited answer
34entities extracted
25relationships
17pages → 17 chunks
4parallel workers
  • Gemini extracts typed entities and relationships as schema-constrained JSON; duplicates are merged by canonical key with idempotent Cypher MERGE upserts.
  • Retrieval uses seed entities, 1–2 hop Cypher traversal and a keyword fallback.
  • Dashboard with an interactive graph, chunk browser and Q&A panel, plus REST APIs.
  • Known limit: charts and image-only slides are not read yet. Embeddings, evaluation and Docker are next.
